DNS Filtering, Ad blocking, & Parental Controls

Control internet access and block unwanted content.

See methods & options

There's multiple approaches to content moderation depending on what you want to moderate, and how much flexibility you want:

Network-Level Filtering:
Configure your router to block domains across your entire home network. Either using firewall settings in your router, or changing router DNS settings. Good for full coverage and simplicity. Your wifi will stop letting devices visit youtube or whatever domain you want.

Device-Level Filtering (NextDNS):
If you don't want certain people accessing certain apps and websites, use services like NextDNS to change DNS settings on individual devices, like smart tvs, phones, etc, and apply urls you don't want them visiting to a denylist. This allows filtering without affecting everyone on the network.

Browser-level Ad Blocking
If you just want to block ads (or any content you'd expect to be on a webpage) Ublock origin and firefox, or brave browser may be your friend. Brave has adblocking baked into the browser. Firefox + Ublock origin has always been my go to for adblocking. The Ublock extension also gives the ability to block elements on the webpage. So you can select a whole div, and have ublock remove it before the site loads.
